How this was built

  • Source: the City Council meeting-minutes PDFs from the city's Legistar site (stpaul.legistar.com), January 7 through October 28, 2026, parsed line by line from each roll call. Closed sessions record no votes; the November 4 meeting had no minutes yet.
  • Each roll-call vote is one row, including procedural motions (closing hearings, layovers, amendments). A consent agenda adopted in one vote counts as one vote; "Agenda items decided" counts the items inside it, less any pulled for a separate vote.
  • "Absent" means the member isn't listed as voting on that item, whether absent for the meeting, arriving late or leaving early. Johnson cast no votes in January and missed most of February's.
  • A motion to deny that every member supported is recorded by the clerk as all "nay" with the item failing; those count as unanimous, not split. Votes reported only as "approved 7-0" in narrative text (some motions to close a hearing) have no roll call and aren't counted.
  • Each vote's yea count was checked against the clerk's tally, and every split vote against the minutes text. The full vote-by-vote file is in Sources.
