Platform sources
    How this is measured
    • Platforms: each member's stated campaign priorities from news coverage, candidate questionnaires and endorsement profiles (Coleman: 2025 special election; others: 2023). Links above. Wording is paraphrased.
    • Record: City Council minutes, January 7 – October 28, 2026. Items are sorted into themes by their titles; one item can fit several themes.
    • Shade: motions, amendments and remarks for or against items in the theme. “Leading” = 4 or more and a bigger share of the member’s activity than the council average; “Active” = 4 or more, or at least 2 above average; “Some” = 1–3.
    • Most items pass unanimously, so votes show little difference between members; activity in debate shows more. Members often move items in their own ward.
    • This compares stated priorities with recorded activity. It does not judge whether a promise was kept: much council work happens in committees, budgets and outside meetings.
