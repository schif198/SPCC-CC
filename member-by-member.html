How this is measured
  • Source: the City Council minutes PDFs (stpaul.legistar.com), January 7 – October 28, 2026. Votes come from each roll call; motions and remarks come from the minutes' narrative.
  • Nearly every vote was unanimous, so differences between members show up mainly in split votes, motions, and remarks recorded against an item or raising concerns.
  • Pushback counts times a member spoke against an item, raised concerns, or moved to delay or deny. Motions counts motions to approve, amend or deny. Both meters are scaled to the most active member in the selected months.
  • Topic focus compares a member's share of motions and remarks on a topic with the council's overall share. Members usually move items in their own ward, so it partly reflects geography.
  • Agreement uses only split votes both members took part in, so in short month ranges it can rest on very few votes, or none.
  • These are patterns in the public record, not ideological labels.
