How this was built
  • Chamber sources: 2024–2026 Strategic Plan (purpose, priorities, core values, strategies) and 2026 Report to Our Community (2026 priorities, local government wins, PAC endorsement). The 2025–26 Policy Platform referenced in the report was not supplied, so specific Chamber positions beyond these documents are NOT AVAILABLE.
  • Council sources: St. Paul City Council minutes PDFs (stpaul.legistar.com), January 7 – October 28, 2026: roll-call votes, motions and remarks.
  • Stances and recommended approaches are Claude's recommendations drawn from each member's public record (votes, motions, remarks, voting partners) and the Chamber's stated values. They describe how to engage, not how a member will vote.
  • Topic search matches the words typed against issue keywords and 2026 item titles, then assembles a plan from the matching issue areas: Chamber priority and framing, members who lead on the issue, members who dissented or pushed back on related items, and council timing. A topic with no match in the record is marked NOT AVAILABLE.
  • Recommendations assume lawful, transparent advocacy: meetings, testimony, written comments, coalition-building and data. Follow the City of Saint Paul's lobbyist registration and reporting rules, and keep PAC activity separate from official requests.
